# Final Publication Readiness Review: Pulmonology, Genetics, and Statistics

**Manuscript Title:** Genetic Factors Influencing Asthma Exacerbation Frequency: A Systematic Review of Candidate Gene Polymorphisms

As the final editorial panel, we have reviewed the manuscript. The authors have done an outstanding job addressing the complex biological, clinical, and statistical feedback. The manuscript is now exceptionally robust.

However, before the manuscript can be formally accepted and sent to typesetting, a few critical inconsistencies introduced by the recent iterative updates must be harmonized.

## 1. Title Restrictiveness vs. Content
* **The Issue:** The current title specifies "A Systematic Review of Candidate Gene Polymorphisms". However, the manuscript now extensively discusses GWAS data, the 17q21 Linkage Disequilibrium block, Polygenic Risk Scores (PRS), and dynamic epigenetic Gene x Environment interactions.
* **Recommendation:** The title should be broadened to reflect the actual scope of the paper. We suggest: "Genetic Architecture of Asthma Exacerbation Frequency: A Systematic Review of Susceptibility Loci and Pharmacogenes".

## 2. Abstract Omissions
* **The Issue:** The abstract explicitly lists the evaluated SNPs: "*ADRB2, IL4, IL4RA, TNFA, NR3C1, PPARG, TGFB1, IL10, CHI3L1, GSDMB, CTNNA3, ADIPOQ, and SEMA3D*". It currently omits the crucial pharmacogenes (*FCER2* and *GLCCI1*) that were recently added to the main text and biological model.
* **Recommendation:** Update the abstract to include *FCER2* and *GLCCI1* in the list of evaluated genes to ensure consistency with the main text.

## 3. PRISMA Diagram and Search Strategy Numbers
* **The Issue:** The authors recently updated the search strategy (Table S1) to explicitly include *FCER2*, *GLCCI1*, and specific EFO trait codes for the GWAS Catalog. However, the PRISMA diagram (Figure 1) still states the exact same number of initial records identified ("n = 2,543") and final included studies ("n = 160") as it did before these new search terms were added.
* **Recommendation:** Statistically, adding new search terms and manually sourcing older literature (the 2011/2013 papers) changes the denominator. The authors must update the numbers in the PRISMA flow diagram to reflect the inclusion of the manual secondary sourcing (e.g., updating "Additional records identified through other sources" from n=12 to n=14) and update the final included studies count (e.g., to n=162). The corresponding text in Section 3.1 must also be updated.

## Summary
Harmonizing the Title, Abstract, and PRISMA numbers with the recently expanded scope of the text will eliminate the final contradictions in the manuscript and ensure it is ready for publication.
